Our Craft

Three rules we never break.

01

Respect the user

No dark patterns, no data hoarding, no paywalls in the doorway. If a feature exists to trick someone, it doesn't ship.

02

Ship, then polish

Release early, listen hard, refine forever. Our best-rated updates were all born in user reviews.

03

Design is the product

Typography, motion, haptics — the details users can't name but always feel. That's where we spend our weekends.
